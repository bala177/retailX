## 📋 Image Generation Platforms Recommendations

### For Professional Quality:

- **Midjourney**: Best for consistent, professional branding
- **DALL-E 3**: Good for specific product shots and variety
- **Stable Diffusion**: Cost-effective for bulk generation
- **Adobe Firefly**: Integration with Adobe tools

### Prompts Tips:

1. **Specificity**: Include exact colors, lighting conditions, and emotions
2. **Style**: Add photography style (e.g., "professional food photography", "medical photography")
3. **Details**: Mention materials, textures, and environmental elements
4. **Quality**: Add "high quality", "sharp focus", "professional lighting"
5. **Mood**: Specify the atmosphere (e.g., "luxurious", "professional", "warm")
